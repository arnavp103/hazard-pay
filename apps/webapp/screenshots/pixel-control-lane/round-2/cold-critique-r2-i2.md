## 1–2. Axis verdicts (round-1 rubric) — vs iteration 1

**1. Gritty / lived-in vs toy-like — BORDERLINE (held).** The figure gained a
little material history at the loupe — a light specular segment on the injector
metal and one or two edge-lit pixels on the boot cuffs — but the *board* is
unchanged: crates still carry structural detail (orange indicator strips, a
cyan display panel, inset panel seams) while every large facet is an immaculate
flat fill and the floating diamond decals keep clean, un-scuffed edges. Grain +
vignette + crate massing still do the anti-toy work. No regression, no real
gain. Held.

**2. Silhouette + equipment readability at wide-tactical zoom — BORDERLINE
(held, marginally improved).** The kit is now genuinely distributed across all
four facings (facing sheet at 8×): the front has a diagonal chest strap + a
white-cross pip + a cyan-tipped injector (the round-1 "empty front hands" is
gone), the sides carry the chest cross + rust pack + injector, and the back is
still the single strongest read — a bold white cross on the rust pack. But at
the actual on-map size (I cropped the medic out of still-wide and blew it up
7×): in the side pose it reads as a hunched teal mass whose reliable cues are
the orange pack and the light injector barrel — the medic-specific white cross
is a 2px reddish smudge that does not resolve. "Medic" from the side at this
distance still leans on the HUD caption. Silhouette-as-a-unit: fine.
Silhouette-as-a-medic from the side: still shaky.

**3. Material separation — BORDERLINE (improved).** This is where the loupe
moved. The iteration-1 "I think I can see a lighter segment but can't confirm a
deliberate specular" is now confirmable: at 8× the injector shows a light-grey/
white highlight cluster on the metal body, distinct from the cyan glow at the
nozzle — metal and emission are now two reads, not one. The boots also pick up
a couple of edge-lit pixels on the cuffs/shins. The one persistent gap: the
hood, torso, sleeves and legs are still a *single* teal material — the darker
teal is form-shading, not a texture or seam break, so the suit is still one
rubberized skin. Improved within BORDERLINE.

**4. Character / environment cohesion — BORDERLINE (held).** The distinct dark
contact shadow still grounds the unit in every frame, and the wide lunge stance
in the attack sits believably on the floor plane rather than floating. Shared
grid, shared palette roles hold. The round-1/iteration-1 float tells also hold:
flat facets, and the diagonal light shaft still passes behind the unit without
spilling a single lit pixel onto its shading. Held.

**5. Motion readability — PASS (improved).** The attack now reads as a decisive
event at a glance, not just "something happened." Across all three attack strips
the head dips, the torso leans in, the injector extends, and a horizontal smear
punches out along the thrust line; in the hybrid the smear terminates in a
bright cyan chevron (a clean Katana-ZERO focal cluster) and the extended pose is
held ~2–3 frames as a hit-pause. This is a clear step up from iteration 1's
"the smear cluster is doing most of the work" — the *body* is now doing the
work and the smear punctuates it. Improved. (Idle motion remains minimal — see
axis 3 below in section 3.)

**6. Deliberate pixel art vs noisy illustration — PASS (improved for the
programmatic path).** The iteration-1 risk lived in the programmatic treatment's
"sub-pixel offset" AA. At a 5× single-frame crop the leaned programmatic frame
now shows a clean 1px ink contour with no grey AA halo — the "snap to whole
pixels" ask appears honored; the frames are on-grid. Authored and hybrid keys
stay crisp. Improved. Honest caveat: static frames cannot reveal *in-motion*
edge shimmer; I can only certify the frames are individually on-grid.

## 3. Round-2 priority axis — does the animation ACT WITH THE WHOLE BODY (incl. legs)? — BORDERLINE (attack CLOSED, idle still OPEN)

This is the decisive axis and it split cleanly. I cropped the lower-body band of
every strip and magnified it 3×.

**ATTACK — the "legs stay planted / half-closed mannequin" verdict is CLOSED,
in all three treatments.** In the neutral loupe the stance is narrow (boots ~1–2px
apart). In the attack, the stance progressively widens into a lunge: by the
extension frames the two boots are clearly separated with a dark triangle of
floor between them, front foot forward, weight spread, then it recovers toward
narrow. This is genuine lower-body articulation, not a lean artifact — the
*inter-leg gap grows*, which a whole-body tilt cannot fake.
- **Authored:** the legs re-pose into a hand-drawn lunge (no shear involved) —
  unambiguous, the cleanest close of the three.
- **Hybrid:** same authored lunge + the brightest impact smear — the fullest
  whole-body action in the set (head dip + torso lean + leg lunge + cyan chevron).
- **Programmatic:** now *also* spreads the stance — the "translated leg-region
  step" reads as a leg block that shifts to widen the base. It moves, so it is no
  longer structurally frozen-legged, but it reads a touch pasted/stiff (a
  translated block rather than a re-articulated stride).
  So the round-1 failure is genuinely reversed for the combat action.

**IDLE — the verdict is still OPEN, in all three treatments.** The idle
leg-bands are static across all five frames in every treatment: planted,
symmetric stance, no foot offset, no hip drop, no weight shift. The idle is an
upper-body sway over dead legs — authored bobs the hood/head, programmatic and
hybrid tilt the whole card forward, but none add a weighted stance. This is now
the isolated pocket of the old "correct pixels, no attitude" tell.

Net: the whole-body directive is answered where it matters most — the attack —
and unanswered in the idle. Because the attack is the decisive combat moment for
an auto-battler, I score the axis BORDERLINE trending PASS, but I will not call
it a clean PASS while the idle legs are still planted.

## 5. Treatment comparison — which best answers the production question now

The relevant canon is Warped City's "readable silhouettes across a large pose
vocabulary," with Katana ZERO punctuation and Magic Pack 9 anticipation/impact.

- **AUTHORED** — now the quality ceiling *and* a real pose vocabulary: it is the
  only treatment that re-draws the legs into a genuine lunge, and its cyan smear
  is clean. *Weakest thing:* sparse (6 stepped keys), highest per-unit cost, and
  the motion between keys can still read steppy; coverage-across-many-units
  remains the open question it can't answer.
- **PROGRAMMATIC** — the story changed: with the added leg-region translation it
  is **no longer disqualified by frozen legs**, and its frames are on-grid now, so
  it is a viable cheap path in a way iteration 1 said it structurally could not
  be. *Weakest thing:* it's still one silhouette sheared + a translated leg block,
  so the lunge reads pasted rather than articulated, the smear is greyer/less
  energetic, and it fundamentally cannot invent a new limb pose (raised arm,
  crouch, reach) — its ceiling is lower even though its floor rose.
- **HYBRID** — still the best overall answer: authored keys give the real
  whole-body lunge, the in-betweens cut frame count, and it lands the strongest
  impact punctuation (the bright cyan chevron). *Weakest thing:* most complex
  pipeline; interpolated in-betweens still risk mush between the crisp keys; and
  it inherits the same waist-up idle.

**Recommendation:** HYBRID best answers "authored vs programmatic vs hybrid" — it
now delivers a genuine whole-body attack (legs included) with the best
punctuation at lower key-count than pure authored. The meaningful change since
iteration 1 is that PROGRAMMATIC is now a legitimate cheap fallback rather than a
dead end, which gives the taste gate a real cost/quality spectrum to choose on.

## 6. Overall read

**Would I now stake on this as an *acting* lane? Yes — for combat, on the hybrid
path (with programmatic as a viable cheap fallback).** The failure that blocked
two straight rounds — the mannequin whose legs never move — is genuinely closed
for the attack across all three treatments. The attack is now a legible
whole-body event: head dips, torso commits, legs lunge, and a comic smear
punctuates the impact. The drawing quality survived the changes intact, and the
programmatic frames came back on-grid. That is the core question answered.

**Has it plateaued?** Not in a bad way — it has *converged* on a working method.
The attack is essentially at "polish only." Further iteration would move the
needle in exactly one place: the **idle**.

**Single biggest remaining risk: the idle.** In an auto-battler the idle is the
most-seen state, and it is still a waist-up sway over planted, symmetric legs
with an unverified loop — the round-1 "correct pixels, no attitude" tell, now
isolated there. If the idle reads lifeless or pops on the loop, the lane will
feel half-alive even though its attack is solved. Secondary risk: medic identity
at the wide zoom still leans on the caption from the side.

## 7. Improvement notes + iterate-or-ship call

Concrete pixel-level punch list:

1. **Give the idle a weighted stance and a verified clean loop.** Offset one boot
   1–2px, drop one shoulder 1px, let the hood hunch asymmetrically, and add a slow
   2–3px breathing rise/fall that *returns* within the cycle. This is the one
   change that would close the whole-body verdict completely (it's already closed
   for the attack). Only reachable in the authored/hybrid paths.
2. **Add a real anticipation frame to the attack.** One pre-thrust frame where
   the injector and torso coil *back* (and the front foot un-loads) before the
   lunge. The hit-pause is already there; the wind-up is not.
3. **Re-earn the front/side medic cross at wide zoom.** Bump the chest cross to a
   3–4px high-contrast white cluster and hold one brighter cyan pixel at the
   injector tip, so "medic" resolves from the side without the caption.
4. **Firm up the programmatic lunge.** If programmatic is chosen, hand-touch 2–3
   pixels on the translated leg block per key so it reads as a stride, not a
   pasted block — cheap, and it removes the "stiff" tell.
5. **Finish the two lingering material notes.** Break the single-material suit
   with 2–3 texture/seam pixels on the shoulder ridge or thigh, and keep the
   injector specular you added.
6. **Scuff the board at the sprite's register.** Nick a few diamond-decal edges
   and drop cracked-seam pixels along a couple of grid lines so the environment
   stops out-cleaning the character.

**Iterate again, or hand to the taste gate?** **Hand it to the human taste
gate now** — with one scoped follow-up flagged. The attack has cleared the bar
that blocked two prior rounds, and the treatment shootout gives the gate a
genuine, legible cost/quality choice (authored ceiling / hybrid best-overall /
programmatic now-viable-cheap). The remaining work is polish, not method risk —
and it is dominated by a single bounded task (make the idle act below the waist).
I would not spend another full iteration before the gate weighs in; let the gate
pick the treatment, then fix the idle on the chosen path.
