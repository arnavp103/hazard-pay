**Read of the previous gap.** r2-i2 named GRITTY (3) as the wall — structures clean, wear not
ON the facades — plus: mute the teal floor spill, add an attack hit-stop + contact spark, push
a clearer turn hop / head-lead. r2-i3 closes most of these. Wear is now on the facades, the
attack genuinely stops, the head leads the turn, and the floor spill is dimmed. Two things are
only partially closed: the turn *hop* and the spill *hue*.

## 1. Gritty / dangerous / lived-in — 4/5 (IMPROVED from 3)
The clean-box read is broken. On the hero combat still the left stall facade now carries
grime/rust streaks running down the wall, a lighter patched panel inset low, dark punched
window openings, and a darker base band where wall meets floor (loupe crop confirms on-surface
marks, not trim). The A/B board shows the vocabulary clearly: decal = painted streaks, chip =
geometric notches + replaced panels, both = notched awning corner + patched panel + streaks.
Combined with muted plum massing, the knocked-over drum and the chemical spill, the register
reads gritty/dangerous, not toy-like. Why not 5: wear is a light dusting versus the Quasimorph
density target — large flat wall planes still read graphic, and the hero shipped a lighter grit
than the fuller "both" A/B option. POLISH ceiling, not structural.

## 2. Silhouette + equipment readability — 4/5 (held)
At combat zoom (~70px, on target) the medic reads instantly: hooded head, backpack hump, orange
shoulder pad, medkit cross, injector-in-hand, distinct stepped boots. Equipment separates.
Capped at 4 by the low-poly box rig — stubby arms, hands that resolve as orange blobs, a fairly
generic blocky torso. STRUCTURAL limit of the rig, out of scope to fix in a prototype.

## 3. Material separation — 4/5 (held)
Cloth (matte plum hood/torso), metal (steel toe-caps read bright white even at combat zoom;
injector body + kit clips), and kit (orange pouches/medkit, teal accents) stay in clean cel
clusters. Metal survives the hero zoom via the toe-caps and injector. Not 5 because metal is
thin outside those two and the flat facets show no material micro-detail — a rig ceiling.

## 4. Cohesion — 4/5 (held; spill improved but not resolved)
The unit owns the chroma peak: character teal collar ~#529189 (value ~57%) and orange shoulder
~#E88F6C are the most saturated/brightest marks against a muted mauve/dark-plum environment.
The r2-i2 note landed — the floor spill was pushed to ~#28524F (value ~32%, ~half the unit's
teal), so it no longer rivals the unit. Residual: it's still the same teal-green hue as the
medic's identity color, so it draws secondary attention, most visibly in the pulled-back 0.82
framing. Dimming worked; the hue collision remains.

## 5. Motion — 4/5 (held; attack + head-lead improved, hop unmet)
- Idle: alive — ~4px breathing bob + gentle injector-hand sway across 24 frames.
- Turn (37f): genuinely acted. Uneven dwell (long front hold f1-7, long back hold f15-23) with
  fast rotation between; a clear crouch STEP through both transitions (deep knee bend, stepping
  toe-cap); a settle back to idle. Head-lead now unambiguous — the hood rotates to profile ahead
  of the torso squaring up (f7-8, f24-25). BUT the vertical HOP is not there: feet stay planted;
  it's a grounded crouch/weight-shift pivot, not an airborne pop. Reads weighty (arguably fine
  for a medic), but the specific hop ask is unmet.
- Attack (22f): the big fix landed. Anticipation coil with a teal charge building (f5-7),
  committed forward-and-down pitch (f9-10), a bright pale-cyan injector SMEAR at the strike
  (f9), then a clear HIT-STOP — the pitched-forward extension holds nearly identical across
  f12-15 before recovery. "Lands but doesn't STOP" is fixed. The contact spark folds into the
  smear + held emitter tip rather than a distinct burst (there's no target to strike), so it
  reads as a swing-flash, not a crisp spark. Capped at 4 by the missing hop and rig limb
  expressiveness.

## Grit A/B recommendation — BOTH, chip-led
Chips (notched corners, replaced/patched panels) alter silhouette and value blocks, so surface
history survives at combat zoom and the pulled-back 0.82 framing where fine decal streaks
flatten toward the wall. Decals alone read as thin painted-on grime and wash out at distance;
chips alone look like clean geometry edits without dirt. Chip-led geometry carries the read far,
decal grime adds density up close — Quasimorph register without its monochrome oppression. Push
the hero render toward the fuller "both" density the A/B board shows, and extend to props.

## VERDICT — PASS, at the threshold
All five axes sit at 4, no axis below 4, and the register genuinely reads gritty/dangerous/
lived-in at combat zoom. It clears the stated bar. Be honest that gritty is the thinnest 4 —
wear is present but restrained versus the density anchor, and props are barely touched. The
remaining gap is mostly a POLISH gap (denser facade wear + scuffed props + a real turn hop +
a crisper contact spark + shifting the spill hue), all closable in one more pass. The only
STRUCTURAL limit is the low-poly box rig itself — stubby arms/hands, thin metal surface, no hop
expressiveness — which caps any axis at 5, and that is correctly out of scope for a prototype.

## Strongest problems
1. Gritty sits at a low 4 — the hero shipped a lighter grit than the fuller both/chip-led
   density; large flat wall planes still read graphic. Push more base dirt-line, climbing
   stains, and patched/chipped panels into the hero render.
2. Props under-worn — the crates/drum are still fairly clean; scuff/dent/stain them.
3. Turn hop absent — the facing change is a grounded crouch/weight-shift pivot with feet
   planted; the requested vertical lift/snap never happens (head-lead + crouch step are present).
4. Teal floor-spill hue still collides with the unit's identity teal — dimmed but same hue
   family; shift its hue (sickly amber/oily chemical) rather than only lowering value.

## Must not be lost
1. The muted plum-black palette discipline with the unit owning the chroma peak (orange+teal
   identity brightest over a muted environment; no magenta; chartreuse confined to the HUD label).
2. The attack's new hit-stop hold + committed pitch + bright cyan injector smear.
3. The turn's uneven dwell + crouch step + head-lead + settle, and the metal toe-caps + injector
   that survive combat zoom for the material read.
